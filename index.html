Mountain-flow advisory

Relative terrain forcing over a sampled elevation grid, from one forecast wind. It is a proxy for where the ground forces the air — not a forecast of vertical velocity, not a turbulence magnitude, and it cannot locate a rotor. Low forcing is not a statement that an area is safe.

What this was computed from

Two baselines: B1 classifies every cell by the terrain forcing the wind imposes on it (w* = V·∇h, a relative proxy), and B2 reads a Froude-like stability regime off the forecast sounding. Surface-driven — it describes flow forced by the ground, not the air at flight altitude.

Stated limitations

    Model: momentum-theory rotor power on total disc area, humid-air density (elevation · temp · humidity), per-chemistry discharge simulation with internal-resistance sag and cold-weather derating off the pack’s own temperature, wind-split cruise optimization per leg. Calibrated to logged flights (see README). Physics is a planning estimate — always fly with margin.
