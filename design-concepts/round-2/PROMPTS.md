# Round 2 ImageGen prompt set

All assets used built-in ImageGen with the first-round concept sheets as visual
references. The shared specification was a shippable FPV field-planning UI with
near-black graphite surfaces, electric-blue planned routes/actions, mint safe
states, amber cautions, large touch targets, restrained overlays, and realistic
Austin/MOZ7/NAV 5000 mission data.

## 05 — Deep mobile mission flow

Generate one landscape contact sheet with exactly four portrait phones in a
coherent sequence: Austin route overview with waypoints and visible wind vectors;
selected-leg editor with distance, course, altitude, speed, shot intent and wind
effect; terrain/link clearance plus wind at 10/80/120/180 m; and mission validation
with energy allocation, turn-home time, landing state, reserve limit, caution
reason and recommended fix. Preserve 3.7 mi, 11:35, 9 mph from SSW/192°, gust 14,
turn home 7:05, land 26%, MOZ7 and NAV 5000. Avoid dense text and sci-fi styling.

## 06 — Mobile wind intelligence

Generate one landscape contact sheet with exactly four portrait phones: Now and
forecast with flying-height versus surface wind and source freshness; vertical
wind profile for 10/80/120/180 m; heading-exposure polar view emphasizing the
home-leg headwind; and a topographic wind map with streamlines, route ring, crest
gain and gap/turbulence cells. Use surface 7 mph S, 80 m 9 mph SSW/192°, 120 m
12 mph SSW, 180 m 11 mph SSW, gust 14 mph, planning wind 11 mph, and 3.7 mi reach.
Use real directional arrows, concise legends, and readable meteorological visuals.

## 07 — Tablet mission studio

Generate one 2x2 landscape tablet contact sheet as a sequential authoring flow:
dominant satellite route canvas with launch, subject, waypoints, rings and wind;
selected Orbit Reveal leg inspector with speed, altitude, camera, subject,
headwind-to-ground-speed conversion and energy effect; aligned route timeline,
per-leg Wh/speed, altitude and wind-exposure simulation; and final GO WITH
CAUTION review with wind, terrain and link evidence plus export actions. Use the
same 3.7 mi, 11:35, land 26%, reserve-to-9-mph mission state throughout.

## 08 — Wind-aware cross-device system

Generate one 2x2 cross-device sheet with two portrait mobile and two landscape
tablet screens. Apply the same persistent wind ribbon to all four: speed, compass
direction, altitude and gust. Show a mobile overview, mobile selected-leg planner,
tablet route studio, and tablet preflight review. The map uses subtle SSW wind
arrows, blue planned and mint best-range rings. Preserve CAUTION, 3.7 mi, 11:35,
turn home 7:05, land 26%, surface 7 mph S, 80 m 9 mph SSW/192°, gust 14, 120 m
12 mph, and 180 m 11 mph. Keep one dominant task per screen and avoid hidden wind,
giant headers, microtext, desktop tables, and decorative HUD effects.
