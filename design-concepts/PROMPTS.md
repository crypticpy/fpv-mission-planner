# Image generation prompts

All four sheets used the built-in ImageGen route and real values observed in the
current FPV Mission Planner.

## 01 — Mobile mission/home

Recompose a landscape 2x2 contact sheet so each device is a clearly portrait,
upright modern phone. Preserve four distinct directions: verdict-first dashboard;
map-first with a compact bottom sheet; modular cards with a radial range gauge;
and a timeline/checklist with a top telemetry strip and reachable bottom action.
Retain small A-D labels and the exact operational strings CAUTION, 3.7 mi, 11:35,
9 mph, 80 m, MOZ7, and NAV 5000. Use near-black instrument-panel styling, amber
caution, restrained blue interactions, large touch targets, safe areas, and
persistent bottom navigation. Avoid paragraphs, microtext, decorative imagery,
marketing styling, and landscape-phone proportions.

## 02 — Mobile map

Create one 16:9 contact sheet with four mobile-first map screens in a 2x2 grid.
Each uses a realistic satellite map with a solid blue planned-radius ring, dashed
green best-range ring, launch pin, and the exact telemetry CAUTION,
9 mph · SSW · 192°, and 3.7 mi. Explore: compact floating overlays; a collapsible
risk card; a map-first bottom sheet; and a segmented tool tray. Preserve safe
areas, one-handed controls, readable telemetry, and practical proportions. Avoid
long prose, desktop sidebars, charts, cyberpunk styling, and obscured map data.

## 03 — Mobile setup/loadout

Create one polished landscape contact sheet with four portrait Setup/Loadout
screens in a 2x2 grid. Explore: guided stepper; compact accordion; editable
summary cards; and a one-handed bottom-sheet/chip pattern. Include Austin, TX
live weather, GEPRC MOZ7 V2 O4 Pro, Lumenier NAV 5000 6S Li-Ion, Naked GoPro,
Long-range cruise, and a 20% landing floor. Prioritize completion, progressive
disclosure, thumb-zone actions, large targets, and concise status. Avoid dense
settings, charts, maps, paragraphs, and desktop layouts.

## 04 — Tablet workspaces

Create one landscape 2x2 tablet contact sheet using the current tablet map as a
product reference. Direction A is a landscape split cockpit with map and compact
mission status. B is a portrait field briefing with verdict, radius, timer, map,
and expandable details. C is a landscape route studio with edge-to-edge map,
route timeline, and leg inspector. D is a compare-and-decide workspace with map
and three compact battery/mission cards. Use CAUTION, 3.7 mi, 11:35, 9 mph from
SSW at 80 m, MOZ7, NAV 5000, the blue planned ring, green best-range ring, and
launch pin. Keep a dark graphite field-instrument system with amber, blue, and
mint accents. Avoid giant headers, tiny walls of text, glow, and transplanted
desktop sidebars.
